Title: Forensic Accountant Job Description

Responsibilities (verbatim excerpts):
- "Conduct forensic accounting investigations and analyses to identify potential fraud, embezzlement, and other financial crimes"
- Gathering and examining financial documentation like statements, tax returns, and bank records
- "Prepare detailed reports documenting findings and recommendations for further action"
- Serving as an expert witness in court proceedings
- Working alongside law enforcement, legal counsel, and other investigation specialists
- Maintaining adherence to accounting standards and regulations

Required Skills & Qualifications (verbatim excerpts):
- "Bachelor's degree in accounting, finance, or a related field"
- CPA or CFE certification preferred
- Minimum 5 years in forensic accounting or similar work
- Deep understanding of accounting principles and methodology
- Proficiency with forensic software platforms like IDEA or ACL
- "Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills"
- Strong organizational capabilities and deadline management
- "Excellent communication and interpersonal skills"
