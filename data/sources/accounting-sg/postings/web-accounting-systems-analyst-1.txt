Title: Accounting Systems Analyst Job Description - ERP | Elevatus
Retrieved: 2026-07-11T08:14:13Z

Responsibilities:
- Overseeing accounting systems including setup, upkeep, and technical assistance
- Supporting ERP rollouts and accounting module system enhancements
- Refining accounting workflows and processes
- Spotting ways systems could work better and creating remedies
- Educating accounting teams on system capabilities and efficient practices
- Managing permissions, protection, and financial data accuracy
- Reviewing system information and fixing accounting platform complications
- Creating and streamlining accounting reports through automation
- Partnering with IT on support requirements and system improvements
- Recording system setups, processes, and accounting procedures

Requirements and Skills:
- Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, Information Technology, or Business Administration
- 3+ years of accounting experience with strong hands-on ERP system knowledge (SAP, Oracle, NetSuite preferred)
- Technical proficiency and comfort customizing system settings
- Background in ERP deployments or system upgrades
- Analytical thinking and systematic problem-solving abilities
- Clear communication abilities, particularly translating technical concepts
- SQL knowledge or report creation tool experience preferred
- Knowledge of accounting processes and industry standards
- Capability collaborating across finance and technology departments
