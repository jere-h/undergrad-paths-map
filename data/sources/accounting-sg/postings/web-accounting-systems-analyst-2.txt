Title: ERP Business Analyst Job Description | Velvet Jobs
Retrieved: 2026-07-11T08:14:13Z

Key Responsibilities:
- Provide guidance and leadership to associate analysts in process analysis, configuration and implementation methodologies
- Review support requests and resolve them independently
- Capture detailed requirements for SAP blueprinting and implementation
- Collaborate with management on process implementation
- Develop business reviews and management reporting
- Optimize relationships between business processes and systems
- Investigate business functions and data structures

Finance/Accounting Specific Requirements (aggregated from multiple real postings on the site):
- Experience in SAP FI and CO modules including General Ledger, Fixed Assets, Management Accounting, and reporting
- Knowledge of Oracle ERP finance modules, specifically General Ledger and sub ledgers
- Strong technical background with Hyperion Planning (EPPM 11.1.2.2 or higher)
- Finance function support experience preferred

Required Skills & Experience:
- 2-10 years of relevant ERP experience depending on position level
- SAP MM/SD module configuration knowledge
- Ability to identify, capture and translate business requirements for technical teams
- Process analysis and streamlining capabilities
- Strong communication and stakeholder management skills
- Cross-functional knowledge development
