REQUIREMENTS & QUALIFICATIONS

Educational Background:
- Currently pursuing a Bachelors, Master's, or PhD degree in a quantitative field
  (Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Physics, Economics, Operations
  Research, or Engineering). Expected graduation Fall 2026 or Spring 2027.

Technical Skills Required:
- Programming proficiency in Python or R for model development and analysis
- SQL for data manipulation and analysis
- Statistical & ML methods: regression, classification trees, unsupervised
  learning, causal inference, time series analysis

Professional Competencies:
- Strong problem-solving skills and ability to work in ambiguous environments
- Collaborative mindset and eagerness to learn in a fast-paced, team-oriented setting
- Clear communication for technical and non-technical audiences

Key Responsibilities:
- Collaborate across teams (Data Scientists, Engineers, Product, Sales, Marketing,
  Finance, Customer Success)
- Apply statistical and machine learning techniques to generate insights or deploy models
- Manage your own project end-to-end - from data exploration and analysis to
  presenting findings
- Contribute to internal data product improvements
